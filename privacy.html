Privacy Policy for Athan Alarm

Package: com.masalma.athanalarm
Effective Date: October 19, 2026 | Last Updated: October 19, 2026

Athan Alarm ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is firmly committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the Athan Alarm mobile application (the "App") for Android and iOS devices.

Summary: Athan Alarm is engineered as a 100% offline, privacy-first application. All prayer calculations, Qibla compass readings, and alarm notifications are computed locally on your device. We do not operate tracking servers, we do not require account registration, and we never sell, share, or monetize your data.

1. Information We Collect and Process

A. Location Data

  • Precise and Approximate Location: The App requests access to your device's location (GPS coordinates, network location) exclusively to calculate astronomical solar positions for Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha) and to calculate the spherical great-circle Qibla bearing toward the Holy Kaaba in Mecca.
  • Strictly On-Device Processing: Your location coordinates are processed in real-time strictly within your device's memory and local storage. Your geographical coordinates are never transmitted over the internet, uploaded to remote cloud servers, or shared with third parties.
  • Manual Option: Users who do not wish to grant location permissions may select a pre-populated city from the offline database or manually input latitude and longitude coordinates.

B. Device Preferences & Local Settings

  • Local Storage: Your chosen preferences (calculation fiqh methods, juristic shadow settings, selected athan sound tones, minute offsets, and alarm modes) are stored locally on your device via standard Android SharedPreferences / DataStore. This information never leaves your device.

2. Information We DO NOT Collect or Share

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, financial information, or device identifiers (such as Advertising IDs or IMEI numbers).
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, sign in, or provide credentials to use any feature of Athan Alarm.
  • No Third-Party Analytics or Trackers: We do not integrate third-party tracking SDKs, marketing trackers, or telemetry beacons.
  • No Data Monetization or Selling: We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute user data to data brokers, ad networks, or commercial entities under any circumstances.

3. How We Use Information

The minimal data processed on-device is used solely for the App's essential functional capabilities:

  • Computing accurate daily prayer and congregation schedules based on astronomical location formulas.
  • Scheduling exact local system alarms and playing selected Athan audio alerts at prayer times.
  • Calculating magnetic compass azimuths toward the Kaaba in Mecca for Qibla direction.

4. Android Device Permissions

Athan Alarm requests only the minimal permissions necessary to deliver its core religious utility:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Used to detect your coordinates for local solar prayer time and Qibla calculation.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Used to deliver timely prayer time notifications to your status bar and lock screen.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM: Used to ensure athan audio and notifications trigger at the exact second prayer time arrives, even when the device is in low-power Doze mode.
  • WAKE_LOCK / FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Used to play athan audio smoothly without being killed prematurely by background battery optimization.

You can adjust or revoke permissions at any time through your device's system settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Athan Alarm is suitable for users of all ages, including families and children. Because our application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

6. Data Security

Because all data remains strictly on your device, your information is protected by the native security architecture of your mobile operating system (Android sandboxing, app-private storage, and encryption). We operate no external databases that could be subject to external breaches.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected on this public webpage with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
